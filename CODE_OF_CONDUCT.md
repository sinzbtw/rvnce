# Code of Conduct

Rvnce welcomes people of all backgrounds and levels of experience. Treat others with respect, give constructive feedback, and accept reasonable disagreements about technical choices.

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized remarks, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and spaces where someone represents the project.

Use GitHub's native reporting controls for abusive content. If the incident needs a maintainer's attention, you may open an issue requesting a private way to contact them. Leave names, private information, and incident details out of that public request.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or block accounts based on the incident and its impact. They should handle reports discreetly and share details only with people needed to act on the report.
